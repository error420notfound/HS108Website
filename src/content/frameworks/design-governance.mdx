## Problem

Growing teams create more design decisions than one person can review. Without governance, each team solves local problems in slightly different ways.

The result is inconsistency, slower delivery, and lower trust in the product.

## Inputs

Governance begins with an audit of existing surfaces, repeated decisions, ownership paths, and points where teams commonly disagree.

## Process

The work is to define which decisions are fixed, which can vary, and who owns exceptions.

Components are useful only after those rules are clear.

## Output

The output is a decision system: principles, patterns, component rules, review paths, and documentation that a team can actually use.

## Example

If every product area creates its own empty state, the solution is not just one empty-state component. The team needs a rule for when empty states teach, warn, recover, or route.
