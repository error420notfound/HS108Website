## Problem

Most design requests arrive as solutions. A team asks for a new identity, website, product interface, or campaign because the visible surface appears to be the problem.

SIAM slows the decision down long enough to understand what the symptom is actually pointing toward.

## Inputs

The framework needs business symptoms, customer behavior, existing touchpoints, and a clear business objective.

## Process

SIAM moves through four questions:

- What symptom is visible?
- What uncertainty creates that symptom?
- Which business decision needs to become clearer?
- What design intervention would change that decision?

## Output

The output is not a deliverable list. It is a diagnosis that explains why a specific intervention should exist.

## Example

If a product has low conversion, the answer may not be a prettier landing page. The deeper issue may be that buyers do not understand the category, the difference, or the next step.
