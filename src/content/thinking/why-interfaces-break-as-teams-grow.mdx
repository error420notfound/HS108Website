## Observation

Small teams can maintain consistency through memory and proximity. Larger teams cannot.

As more people make interface decisions, undocumented judgment becomes a liability.

## Analysis

Component libraries solve only part of the problem. Teams also need rules for when to use a pattern, when to create a new one, and how decisions should be reviewed.

Without that layer, teams keep producing new variants because every local problem appears unique.

## Implication

A mature design system should govern decisions, not only store components. It should reduce ambiguity for designers, engineers, and product owners.

## Application

Before adding new components, audit the decisions that created the inconsistencies. Then document the rules that would prevent those decisions from repeating.
