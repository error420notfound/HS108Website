## Observation

Many products work better than they look. The team knows the product is reliable. Existing customers may know it too. New buyers do not.

Trust is not transferred automatically from operations to perception.

## Analysis

When a buyer cannot quickly understand what a product does, why it matters, and what will happen next, uncertainty rises. That uncertainty is often interpreted as risk.

Design cannot manufacture trust by adding polish. It can only make the right evidence easier to find, compare, and believe.

## Implication

Trust problems should be diagnosed before identity or interface work begins. Otherwise teams redesign the surface while leaving the decision problem untouched.

## Application

Look for the moments where a customer has to decide without enough context. Then ask what evidence, sequence, or language would reduce uncertainty at that exact point.
